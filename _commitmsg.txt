Fix: a tester's Accept could go quiet forever holding their money

Found on the first real Accept from a funded test account: two intents on
mill ideas sat pending with $7.12 reserved and no DM, indefinitely.

The loop. bot.on_callback writes a decisions row for every Accept, and
expire_stale_ideas exempted any accepted idea from the 15-minute clock. So a
tester's own Accept pinned the idea in a live status forever, which kept
mill_<id> in pool_active_mill_refs forever, which meant expire_stale_intents
never released the reserve and the card's promise -- "I'll DM you when it
fills or gets pulled" -- was never kept.

That exemption is right for a fill operator, whose Accept is the fill and must
not be expired underneath them, and wrong for a tester, whose Accept is only a
claim on a fill someone else has to make. It is now scoped to
LIVE_MILL_FILL_TELEGRAM_IDS.

Fixing expiry alone would have introduced the opposite error.
pool_active_mill_refs listed only live statuses, but the re-offer sweep
deliberately replays expired ideas within LIVE_MILL_REOFFER_MAX_AGE_MIN after
re-pricing them against the live mark. A reserve released at the 15-minute
expiry could therefore be followed by a fill of that same idea at minute 40,
without the tester who accepted it -- worse than releasing late. It now
includes re-offer-eligible ideas.

New POOL_INTENT_TTL_MIN=150 backstops both, so an Accept always terminates
even if that bookkeeping breaks again. It sits deliberately above the
120-minute re-offer window so the backstop can never race a real fill and drop
a tester out of a trade they were promised.

Accept copy now says a card can rest a couple of hours, because it can, and
silence for that long is expected rather than a fault.

Also: /democard all broadcasts to every approved account, rendering a separate
card per recipient because the size line is personal. A bare /democard only
ever reached one account, which read as a bug.

Full suite green, 1128 passing.
