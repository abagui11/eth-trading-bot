# Round 2 implementation plan — 2026-09-21

Three workstreams out of the weekend analysis (`trade_ideas/analysis/_q0921_*.py`),
plus the operator decision to run live capital on the two leading variant
books ahead of the §4 bar. Ordered so each item names its evidence, its cost,
and what would show it didn't work.

**The one design constraint that shapes everything in workstream C:** the
paper variant books are the *measurement instrument* for the n=60
determination. Live execution must therefore be a mirror beside them, never a
replacement — the moment a variant's paper book starts recording live fills,
the experiment is comparing books under different execution models and the
n=60 readout is contaminated. Control already works exactly this way
(`paper.py` is the book, `execute.py` mirrors live); the variants copy that
pattern.

## Workstream A — conditional read repairs (bug tier + unblock)

Evidence: 580 reads over 3 days, 97% withheld. Null decomposition: 51% no
array detected, 34% array untested, 9% no draw. All 19 emitted biases hung on
one BTC H1 array; 3 carried a bullish bias with the draw *below* the array.

### A1. Draw-side enforcement (bug — no measurement question)
`conditional.assemble_read` checks array-side-vs-bias but not
draw-side-vs-bias. The prompt states the rule and `_programmatic_choice`
enforces it; the LLM path does not. Fix: a bullish bias requires the draw
midpoint above spot, bearish below, else bias dropped with
`dropped_reason="draw on wrong side"`. Test: the three recorded 09-18 reads
as fixtures.

### A2. Read dedup (bug tier — honest counting)
The same setup re-emitted every 30 minutes counted as 19 observations of ~4.
Add `dedup_key = hash(product, tf, bias, array lo/hi, draw lo/hi)`; a row is
inserted only when the key changes or the prior read resolved
(target/invalidated on the M5 path). The scorer scores unique keys. Keeps the
board fresh while the ledger counts setups, not re-prints.

### A3. Widen candidate generation (the unblock)
Four named convention changes, dated in `conditional.py`'s docstring — this is
3 days into collection, so changing conventions now costs almost nothing and
every day of delay wastes window:

1. **H4 arrays visible to H1 reads**, tagged `htf` in the candidate list.
2. **Zone lookback 60 → 120 bars** on the H1 detector call.
3. **Pool tolerance 15 → 30 bps** (`_POOL_TOL_PCT`), and pools also from H4
   swings for H1 reads.
4. **`holding` loosened to "retest"**: price inside the zone *or* within
   0.25×ATR24 of its near edge after having traded away. The 34% `untested`
   bucket is the second-largest null source and strict containment is our
   convention, not ICT doctrine — the change is a choice and is recorded
   as one.

**Expected within 48h (~380 reads):** no-array bucket < 25%, biases emitted
> 15%, more than one array in play, both products represented.
**Falsifier:** if after these four changes the withheld rate is still > 90%,
the schema's null rule itself is the bottleneck and the next move is a design
review, not more loosening.

## Workstream B — Kalshi regime gates

Evidence: full-history weekend split (streak −0.559/trade over 61 weekend
trades vs +0.099 weekday; wick −0.226 over 289 vs +0.375), chop correlation
−0.4 on both directional books, +0.14 on arb. Cooldown sweep: streak positive
in 11/12 cells; wick negative in 9/12.

### B1. Weekend pause on `eva_streak` and `eva_wick`
Skip new entries Sat/Sun UTC (settlement of open positions unaffected).
Evidence tier: full recorded history, both epochs, both books. Would have
avoided +34 (streak) and +65 (wick) net of skipped winners.
**Falsifier:** two consecutive profitable weekends in the shadow log
(the gate keeps computing and logging what it skipped).

### B2. Chop gate — shadow first
The real variable is chop, not the calendar (Friday was a weekday-quality
trend). Compute trailing-24h `range ÷ |net|` per product at decision time; log
`would_gate` above a threshold **without acting** for one week, then pick the
threshold from the shadow log. Shipping a threshold tuned on one weekend is
exactly the noise-fit the house rules prohibit.

### B3. Loss cooldown on `eva_streak` only
k=3 consecutive settled losses → 4h pause per product. The cell is chosen for
consistency with the mill's shipped precedent (`LIVE_MILL_LOSS_COOLDOWN_N=3`,
240 min), and it sits on the sweep's plateau (+11 to +25 across k=2–3,
H=2–24h) rather than at its maximum. **Explicitly not shipped:** any wick
cooldown (negative in 9/12 cells — its losses don't cluster, they're
regime-priced) and any change to `eva_arb` (88% win weekday, positive both
regimes, n too small to touch).

### B4. Deploy mechanics
`/opt/kalshi-15m-bot` is **not a git checkout** — it was deployed by copy.
This workstream includes: push the pending local commit (`765ddc2`), get the
box onto git (clone + `.env` carry-over + service restart) or at minimum a
checksummed copy step, so the next Kalshi change isn't hand-carried. The
uncommitted WIP on the local kalshi repo (bot_config, kalshi_client,
strategies/*) belongs to the operator and is not part of this plan.

## Workstream C — live capital on `eva_swing_llm` and `eva_day`

Operator decision, recorded as such: run the two positive-R books live
*alongside* control, ahead of the §4 promotion bar. This is an **addition,
not a promotion** — control stays `EVA_LIVE_VARIANT`, stays live, and stays
the signal source. Determinations still happen at n=60 on the paper books.

### C1. Prereg amendment (first commit of the workstream)
Dated note in `EVA_VARIANTS_PREREG.md`: live capital extended to
`eva_swing_llm` and `eva_day` on 2026-09-21 as an operator risk decision, not
an evidence decision; the §4 bar is unchanged and still governs *promotion*;
the experiment epoch is unchanged; live results are excluded from the §4
statistics (the paper books remain the measurement). The 9-trade means that
motivated this (+3.05R, +3.28R) are inside one SE of zero and are recorded as
context, not justification.

### C2. Execution routing
On variant open, mirror to live with a distinct source tag:

| Book | Live source tag | What mirrors |
|---|---|---|
| control | `hq` (unchanged) | as today |
| `eva_swing_llm` | `hq_swing` | its own vision plans (already validated by `validate_swing_plan`) |
| `eva_day` | `hq_day` | **`vision_rebracket` only** |

`eva_day`'s `m1_trigger` path is **excluded from live**: the prereg gives it
an explicit negative prior (the watchdog precedent), no backtest exists, and
it has zero closed positions. It stays paper-only inside the same book.

Sizing: fixed dollar risk per clip (`LIVE_VARIANT_RISK_USD`, default = the
same ~$10–15 risk control's clips carry), *not* portfolio-fraction — the
variants' paper books are fixed-risk and the live mirrors should be
recognisable next to them.

### C3. The stacking problem, decided explicitly
`eva_day` re-brackets **control's own entries**. When control fills live and
day mirrors the same entry live, that is 2× live exposure on one idea (3× if
swing_llm independently reaches the same conclusion). Decision: **allow the
stack but cap it** — combined open live risk per (product, side) across all
three sources ≤ `LIVE_VARIANT_STACK_RISK_CAP_USD`. Chosen over "skip day when
control filled" because the books stay independently interpretable and the
cap bounds the concentration; the alternative silently censors day's live
book on exactly the entries it shares with control.

**Same-product opposite directions are a harder constraint.** All sleeves
share one CDE contract; a resting bracket reserves the whole position, so
two sleeves cannot hold opposite sides at once. Operator order
(2026-09-21, after the swing mirror blocked both day and control BTC shorts):
swing > day > control > mill. Same-direction stacking still allowed under the
risk cap above; opposite-direction conflict is resolved by rank, not by
whoever fired first. Cost: control's live book is subordinate to both
mirrors — recorded in the prereg amendment, not hidden.

### C4. Kill-switches
Per-book live flags (`EVA_SWING_LLM_LIVE_ENABLED`, `EVA_DAY_LIVE_ENABLED`),
both revertible without touching the paper books or the epoch. Turning one
off is one flag; the experiment never notices.

### C5. Dashboard + docs
- **Eva Lab tab:** live badge on the two books; the candidate-only framing
  stays (a live badge is not a verdict, and the tab should say so).
- **Eva Trades tab:** P&L split by source across `hq` / `hq_swing` / `hq_day`
  — three columns, one combined line, no blending into a single number
  (blending is how the paper book's +0.882R fiction happened).
- **Investor page:** live positions gain a source label; combined equity
  unchanged in meaning.
- `PROJECT_STATE.md` §8/§9 + changelog; `CLOUD.md` if flags land in `.env`.

### C6. What would show this was a mistake
Stated now, checked at the §6 review: if at n=60 either book fails the §4
bar, the live mirror shuts off and the live P&L it accumulated is reported in
the changelog as the cost of running ahead of the evidence — not netted away,
not re-based.
